\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\markboth{Abstract}{}
\selectlanguage{english}

Moroccan citizens still struggle to access legal information: the texts are public, but written
in dense legal language, structured as hundreds of cross-referencing articles, and sometimes
available only in Arabic. General-purpose conversational assistants do not fill this gap: they
know Moroccan law poorly, may invent non-existent references --- a plausible but wrong article
number is particularly misleading in a legal setting --- and send citizens' questions outside
the national territory.

This internship covered the design, implementation and evaluation of a \textbf{sovereign} legal
search assistant built on the RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architecture, applied to the
Moroccan Labour Code (Law no.~65-99). The system answers a natural-language question using
\emph{only} the articles actually retrieved from the corpus, systematically cites their numbers,
and explicitly abstains when the requested information is not in the text. The entire chain ---
corpus, embeddings, retrieval and generation --- runs locally, with no external API call.

The corpus was built from the official PDF: \NbArticles{} articles extracted, cleaned and
enriched with their hierarchy metadata, with regeneration verified byte-for-byte identical from
the source. Retrieval combines a semantic method (BGE-M3 embeddings, Chroma vector store) with a
lexical one (BM25), fused by Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Generation uses a Mistral~7B model running
locally via Ollama, constrained by two model-independent guardrails: an abstention threshold
applied \emph{before} the model is called, and a check that every cited article was actually
present in the supplied context.

The system was evaluated on a \NbQuestions-question reference set. In its hybrid configuration,
retrieval reaches Recall@5 of \RecallHybride{} and MRR of \MrrHybride{}. On the answer side,
\TauxCitation~\% cite at least one article, \TauxVerif~\% of citations are verified as genuinely
supplied to the model, and abstention is correct on \TauxAbstention~\% of out-of-scope questions
--- no answer was ever produced on a topic absent from the corpus. Median response time is
\LatTotale~seconds, dropping to \LatAbstention~seconds when the guardrail fires.

A coverage audit, carried out once the system had stabilised, revealed that the initial reference
set exercised none of several code paths. Growing it from 37 to \NbQuestions{} questions
\textbf{reversed} two previously measured conclusions: the comparison between dense and hybrid
retrieval, and the apparent separation of the abstention score bands. This methodological result
--- an evaluation harness is a designed artefact and must be validated like the system it judges
--- is central to this report, alongside the acknowledged measurement limits, chief among them the
inability to automatically verify that a cited article \emph{actually says} what the answer
attributes to it.
