\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Moroccan citizens still struggle to access legal information: the texts are public, but written
in dense legal language and sometimes available only in Arabic. General-purpose conversational
assistants know Moroccan law poorly, may invent non-existent references, and send citizens'
questions outside the national territory.

This internship covered the design, implementation and evaluation of a \textbf{sovereign} legal
search assistant built on the RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architecture, applied to the
Moroccan Labour Code (Law no.~65-99). The system answers a natural-language question using
\emph{only} the articles actually retrieved from the corpus, systematically cites their numbers,
and explicitly abstains when the requested information is not in the text. The entire chain ---
corpus, embeddings, retrieval and generation --- runs locally, with no external API call.

The corpus was built by hand from the official PDF: 589 articles extracted, cleaned and enriched
with their hierarchy metadata, with regeneration verified byte-for-byte identical from the source.
Retrieval combines a semantic method (BGE-M3 embeddings, Chroma vector store) with a lexical one
(BM25), fused by Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Generation uses a Mistral~7B model running locally via
Ollama, constrained by two guardrails: an abstention threshold applied \emph{before} the model is
called, and a check that every cited article was actually present in the supplied context.

The system was evaluated on a 37-question reference set. Retrieval reaches Recall@5 of 0.97 and
MRR of 0.89 in the dense configuration. On the answer side, 97~\% cite at least one article, 94~\%
of citations are verified as genuinely supplied to the model, and abstention is correct on 100~\%
of out-of-scope questions. Median response time is 3.5~seconds, dropping to 0.14~seconds when the
abstention guardrail fires. Measurement limits --- notably the inability to automatically verify
that a cited article \emph{actually says} what the answer attributes to it --- are documented
explicitly.
